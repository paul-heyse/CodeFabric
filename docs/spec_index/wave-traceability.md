## Milestone map

| Milestone | Proved capability |
|---|---|
| M02 | successor v2.2 authority and independently reviewed expectations |
| M03 | honest production composition plus predecessor semantic-authority zero state |
| M04 | DataFusion/catalog and Delta/durability/recovery closure |
| M05 | exact provider-to-public production delivery through supervisor, v2 RPC, and FastMCP |
| M06 | independent evidence, bounded operations, and total targeted purge |
| M07 | fresh target-only activation and final certification at one trusted HEAD |

No milestone is satisfied by file presence, an executor statement, a state label, a digest, or
predecessor agreement. Its exact packet oracles and decommission exits must pass at an ancestral
proving commit and again at the certification HEAD. WP28 and M01 are absent from v4 and confer no
authority or proof.
